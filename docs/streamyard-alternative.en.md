# CastDeck Local as a StreamYard alternative — with the video call at the center

> **TL;DR:** CastDeck is a live studio that runs on your own computer. It was designed for the same core
> use case as StreamYard — **talking to guests live** — but with local processing, recording on your disk,
> plan-free multistream and open source.
>
> StreamYard is a trademark of its respective owners. This is an independent comparison, not affiliated.

---

## 1. What actually matters in a guest-based live show

Interviewers, podcasters, teachers, churches, sports coverage and talk shows need six things:

1. **Guests join easily.** A browser link, no app download, no account.
2. **Guests hear themselves correctly.** Mix-minus: they hear the program, not their own delayed voice.
3. **Guests see the program.** They react to the live scene, not just their own camera.
4. **The host controls the scene.** Preview, PGM, layouts, screen share, audio and graphics.
5. **The show goes out everywhere.** YouTube, Twitch, Facebook and RTMP/SRT.
6. **Recording is reliable.** A local file that does not depend on a plan or the cloud.

CastDeck addresses those six points with one difference: **the studio runs on your machine**.

## 2. Where CastDeck shines in the video call

| Feature | How CastDeck does it |
|---|---|
| **Invite by link** | Guests open Chrome/Edge on phone or desktop. No account, no install. |
| **Mix-minus** | Each guest receives the program without their own voice; conversation flows naturally. |
| **Program return** | Guests see the live scene in real time, including graphics and layouts. |
| **Stage and layouts** | Host + guests in compositions from 1 to 4+; one-click switching and switcher control. |
| **Sharing** | Screen, tab, media, phone camera via QR and RTMP/SRT inputs. |
| **Pro audio** | Mixer with faders, dBFS meters, A–G buses, noise reduction and talkback. |
| **Local recording** | MP4 on your disk, independent of plan and show length. |
| **Vertical 9:16** | Dedicated layout and recording, simultaneous with 16:9, for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. |
| **Remote control** | Phone becomes a switcher/remote via QR, no install. |

The core point: **the video call is not an accessory to the stream; it is the reason the studio exists.**

## 4. What StreamYard still does better

Honesty is part of the product:

- **Zero installation and zero maintenance.** No PC to update, port to configure or FFmpeg to manage.
- **Official support and SLA.** For companies that need a contract, this matters.
- **Fully cloud-based operation.** Does not depend on your upload to composite the scene.
- **Integrated ecosystem.** Plans, teams and managed features in one place.

If those points are decisive, StreamYard is the right choice. If you want to **own your studio**,
CastDeck is the alternative.

---

## 5. When to choose CastDeck

Choose CastDeck if you:

- run **interviews, podcasts, classes, services, sports or talk shows** with guests;
- want **local MP4 recording** without plan limits;
- need **16:9 and 9:16 at the same time**;
- want **multistream** to several destinations;
- value **privacy** and want video composited on your device;
- accept managing a PC (and maybe a network) in exchange for control;
- want **open source** to study, adapt or integrate.

**Choose StreamYard** if you need fully managed operations, contractual support and do not want to think
about infrastructure.

## 6. Migrating in 10 minutes

1. **Download** CastDeck from [cast.flue.live/downloads.html](https://cast.flue.live/downloads.html).
2. Open the app and **create a studio**.
3. Click **Invite**, copy the link and send it to your guest.
4. Build the scene: cameras, names, graphics, screen share.
5. Add destinations (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, RTMP/SRT).
6. Run a short test: local recording + preview.
7. When comfortable, **go live**.

**Tip:** start with one guest and a simple scene. Then add graphics, vertical and multistream.

---

## 7. Limits and transparency

- **Depends on your computer.** CPU/GPU, RAM and upload matter; 8 GB RAM is recommended.
- **Depends on your network.** Guests behind difficult NAT may need TURN; the app supports it.
- **You manage the app.** Updates, firewall and ports are your responsibility.
- **No 24/7 support by default.** There is a community and an optional commercial license.
- **Open-source model.** AGPL-3.0 requires publishing modifications when distributing/serving the software;
  understand the license before using it in a closed product.
